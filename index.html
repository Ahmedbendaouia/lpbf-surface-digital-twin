What is measured

Every coloured line is a real height profile exported by the microscope: 10 horizontal and 10 vertical lines per spot, 2.08 µm apart along the line at x50. Roughness is computed live from these points with the same method as the workbook. The Ra check under the profile chart compares the two.

What is reconstructed

The 3D relief under the profiles is rebuilt from the microscope's colour height-map image and calibrated against that spot's own profiles. Its fit quality (R², RMS) is shown per spot. Each acquisition has its own height zero and the instrument colour bar is rescaled per image, so every field is levelled to its mean plane and the Height (levelled) skin uses one colour scale for all fields. The map is smoothed by the instrument, so it shows form and waviness, not fine roughness. Plate spacing is schematic.

Method

End padding removed → least‑squares line removed → Gaussian filter (ISO 16610‑21) at the chosen λc, ends trimmed by λc/2 → Ra, Rq, Rz, Rt, Rsk, Rku (ISO 21920‑2). Rz averages the peak‑to‑valley height of each λc‑long segment. Data built by 06_Digital_Twin/build_twin_data.py.
